About Us

A Trusted Leader in
Freight Forwarding

Worth Services Co., Ltd. was established in 2006 as a leading freight forwarding and logistics company headquartered in Yangon, Myanmar. Our young and dynamic management team brings decades of combined expertise in international trade and supply chain management.

We are dedicated to providing best-in-class services at the lowest possible cost — connecting Myanmar businesses with global markets through reliable, transparent, and innovative logistics solutions.

  • Cross-border freight forwarding specialists
  • Customs clearance and MIC application experts
  • Worldwide partner network across 50+ countries
  • Door-to-door tracking and real-time visibility

What We Offer

Our Core Services

From ocean freight to documentation support, we provide end-to-end logistics solutions tailored to your business needs — with a commitment to responsiveness, transparency, and integrity.

Ocean Freight

Cost-effective sea freight options for large volume cargo with full and less-than-container-load options.

Air Freight

Fast and reliable air cargo services for time-sensitive shipments to destinations worldwide.

Clearance Services

Expert customs clearance handling to ensure smooth and compliant import and export procedures.

Warehousing

Secure and modern warehousing facilities with inventory management and order fulfillment services.

Project Cargo

Specialized handling of oversized, heavy, or complex project cargo requiring custom logistics planning.

Door-to-Door Delivery

Complete end-to-end shipping from origin pickup to final destination delivery with real-time tracking.

MIC Application

Permit and MIC (Ministry of Commerce) application assistance for smooth regulatory compliance in Myanmar.

Documentation Support

Comprehensive paperwork assistance including bills of lading, certificates of origin, and customs declarations.
